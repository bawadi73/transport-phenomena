Momentum, heat, and species all spread by the same mechanism and all penetrate a distance proportional to √(diffusivity × time). Start the race and watch three fronts leave the wall together — and arrive nowhere near each other. The gap between them is what the Prandtl, Schmidt, and Lewis numbers actually measure.
